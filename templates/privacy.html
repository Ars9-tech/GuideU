Privacy Policy

How GuideU collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our campus navigation platform.

📅 Last updated: April 2025
🔒
📋 Table of Contents
1 Information We Collect 2 How We Use Information 3 Third-Party Sharing 4 Data Retention & Security 5 Your Rights 6 Changes to This Policy
📡
Section 01

Information We Collect

GuideU relies on location services to help you navigate the campus efficiently. We may collect the following types of information:

  • Your real-time GPS location while using navigation features
  • Device type, browser, and operating system information
  • Login credentials (username, hashed password, or Google OAuth token)
  • Recent navigation history stored locally on your device
  • Profile information from Google Sign-In (name, email, profile picture)
📍 Location data is only accessed when you actively use navigation — we never track you in the background.
⚙️
Section 02

How We Use Information

All collected information is solely used to enhance the GuideU experience on the Bennett University campus:

  • Providing accurate point-to-point GPS navigation across campus
  • Suggesting nearby food outlets, academic blocks, and hostels
  • Maintaining secure account access and saving your preferences
  • Displaying your recent visit history for faster navigation
  • Personalizing greetings and time-based features
🤝
Section 03

Third-Party Sharing

We believe in maintaining your privacy. GuideU does not sell, trade, or transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties without your explicit consent.

  • Google OAuth is used only for authentication — we don't share data back
  • OpenStreetMap & OSRM are used for mapping — no personal data is sent
  • University administration may access data only if required by policy
  • Law enforcement requests are handled strictly per applicable law
🛡️ We use Google Sign-In for convenience only. Your Google data is never stored beyond your name, email, and profile picture.
🔐
Section 04

Data Retention & Security

We take data security seriously and implement industry-standard measures to protect your information:

  • Passwords are hashed using SHA-256 — never stored in plain text
  • Session tokens are secured and expire on logout
  • Location data is processed in real-time and not permanently stored
  • Recent visits are stored only in your browser's localStorage
  • Our database is protected against unauthorized access
👤
Section 05

Your Rights

As a GuideU user, you have full control over your data:

  • You can log out at any time to end your session
  • You can clear your recent visits by clearing browser localStorage
  • You can revoke Google OAuth access from your Google account settings
  • You can request account deletion by contacting the GuideU team
📢
Section 06

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our app or university guidelines. When we make significant changes:

  • Changes will be communicated via your GuideU dashboard
  • The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised
  • Continued use of GuideU after changes constitutes acceptance
✉️

Questions about this policy?

If you have any questions or concerns about how GuideU handles your data, reach out to the development team through the Bennett University student portal or raise an issue on the project repository.
